# Format reference

## Canvas
1080×1920, 30 fps. World camera with three origins (scene 1, scene 2 below, CTA to the right); whip-pans between scenes,
a punch-in on `PUNCH`. Durations come from the voice: each line starts ~0.03 s after the previous one ends.

## Tokens
- bg `#F7F7F5`, grid `#E6E6E3` 1px/60px, vignette 6% top/bottom
- ink `#1A1A1A`, grey `#8A8A8A`, blue `#4A7CF7` (pale `#E8EEFE`), yellow `#FFE45C`, red `#E5484D`, green `#2BB673`, CTA black `#111111`
- Cards: white, radius 28, shadow `0 12 32 rgba(0,0,0,.08)`
- Fonts: Inter Tight 600/700/800 (headlines, numbers), Inter (labels), Instrument Serif Italic (highlighted headline word)
- Headline 92 px at top 290; body numbers 40–60 px; labels 22–28 px letter-spaced caps

## Safe areas
Key content x 60–1020, y 250–1100 for scenes. Captions y ~1480–1580. Avatar ~200 px, bottom-left in scene 1, bottom-right in scene 2.

## Audio
- VO: each line loudness-normalised (−16 LUFS, −2 dBTP), pauses capped at 0.35 s.
- Music ~105 BPM, ducked under VO; SFX −18…−24 dB, never above the voice.
- Master: −14 LUFS integrated, −1.5 dBTP, AAC 320k 48 kHz.

## Compliance
No competitor/teacher names, no university logos, no score or admission promises, "free" only if it is free,
only claims the product page supports. Not affiliated with GMAC — don't imply it.
